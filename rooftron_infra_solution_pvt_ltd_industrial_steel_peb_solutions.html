Corporate Profile • Patna, Bihar

Built to Protect. Engineered to Last.

Rooftron Infra Solution Private Limited is a registered Indian infrastructure, roofing, and structural steel solutions company headquartered at City Centre Mall, Patna, Bihar. We partner with EPC contractors, warehouse developers, factory owners, and government infrastructure agencies to supply precision-engineered steel buildings and cladding systems.

From primary structural framing (built-up I-sections, Z & C purlins) to high-rib colour-coated roofing profiles, deck sheets, highway W-beam crash barriers, and solar mounting structures, every batch undergoes dimensional and coating thickness verification before site dispatch.

01. Custom Design

STAAD.Pro & Tekla-aligned structural detailing tailored for regional wind loads (IS 875) & seismic zones.

02. Mill-Certified Steel

Sourced from prime integrated steel mills with full Mill Test Certificates (MTC) for yield strength & zinc coating.

03. Turnkey Supply

Single-window supply of primary frames, roof sheets, fasteners, flashings, and safety accessories.
